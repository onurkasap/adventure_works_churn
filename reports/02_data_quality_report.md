# AdventureWorks2022 data quality report

## Scope and method

Read-only checks in [01_data_quality_checks.sql](../sql/02_data_quality/01_data_quality_checks.sql) were run against `AdventureWorks2022`. The focus was `Sales.Customer`, `Sales.SalesOrderHeader`, and `Sales.SalesOrderDetail`, with joins on their verified relationships. This is data-quality discovery only: no source rows were changed, no KPIs were calculated, and no churn labels were assigned.

## Churn-related data limitations

- **Finite and aging history:** the observed sales history ends on **2014-06-30**. It is not evidence that customer activity continued to be observed after that date. The final date creates right censoring, especially for customers purchasing near the end of the period.
- **One-purchase customers:** **11,649** customers have exactly one recorded order. They have no observed repeat interval, so a later behavioral churn definition must handle them explicitly.
- **Zero-purchase customers:** **701** customer records have no linked order. They are not “churned after purchase” under a post-first-purchase definition because no first transaction is recorded.
- **Churn is not an observed source label:** these tables record customers and transactions, not a verified customer churn event. Any future churn status will be an analytical label inferred from inactivity over a chosen period, requiring a business definition, an eligible population, and enough follow-up time. It must not be presented as a directly observed fact.
- **Observed orders are not necessarily the whole relationship:** a finite sales extract cannot establish whether an apparently inactive customer bought through another channel or after the extract ended. Confirm source coverage and the business meaning of an order before drawing customer-retention conclusions.

## Recommended starting point for the next phase

Use the verified customer/order/detail keys; keep registered customers without orders distinct from purchasing customers; and explicitly set the observation cutoff and minimum follow-up period before defining retention or churn. Define how order status and the observed line/header amounts will be treated in metric work. This report does not select those business definitions or calculate any KPIs.
